## Description (4000 char max)

RandoSquad takes the guesswork out of grouping students and calling on
them fairly.

Add your roster once, then let RandoSquad do the rest:

RANDOM, BALANCED GROUPS
Generate 2 to 6 groups in one tap. RandoSquad looks at each student's
history — who's grouped with whom, and who's held which role — and
balances new groups against it, so the same pairings and the same roles
don't repeat activity after activity.

FAIR PARTICIPATION, NO FAVORITES
Pick a student to answer with one tap. RandoSquad weights the pick toward
whoever has participated the least, so quieter students get called on too
— and you can log every pick to keep a running participation count per
student.

GRADE BY GROUP
Save any group set as a named activity, then grade each group directly.
Every grade rolls up into simple charts so you can see performance by
activity or by student at a glance.

BUILT FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
One roster, one set of groups, laid out properly on whatever device you
grab — phone in your pocket between classes, iPad on your desk during one.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything lives on your device. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no
tracking, no internet connection required. Your roster is yours.

Whether you're running small-group discussions, lab partners, project
teams, or just making sure every student gets a fair shot to answer,
RandoSquad keeps it quick, fair, and simple.
