# Privacy Policy for RandoSquad

**Last updated: August 23, 2026**

RandoSquad ("the App") is a classroom tool that helps teachers manage
class rosters, build random student groups, track grades, and log
participation.

## Data Collection

RandoSquad does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
The App has no analytics, no advertising, no user accounts, and no network
connection of any kind.

## Data Storage

Any information you enter — student names, ID numbers, email addresses,
participation counts, group assignments, and grades — is stored **only on
your device**, in a local database inside the App's own storage. It is
never sent to us, to Apple, or to any third party. Deleting the App deletes
this data.

## Third-Party Services

RandoSquad does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or
tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

RandoSquad is a tool for teachers and does not knowingly collect any
information from students directly — all data is entered and controlled by
the teacher using the App, and stays on that teacher's device.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the
revised policy.
